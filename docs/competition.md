## Equal-weight judging dimensions and our evidence

Implementation: actual Strands tool use, validated structured output, live run.
Design: one complete handoff flow with clear unresolved items and citations.
Impact: realistic hotel cases checked by the user; do not invent time savings.
Originality: reconcile conflicting handoffs and expose missing information.
Presentation: demonstrate sources, extraction, exceptions, and usable output.

## Acceptance criteria for the first milestone

- An explicit invoice request with missing billing details remains actionable.
- Conflicting completion/open reports remain visible for human review.
- Notes without requests do not create invoice obligations.
- Unknown citations and fabricated quotes fail validation.
- Replay is visibly distinguished from real model execution.

These checks do not measure extraction recall or semantic faithfulness. Live model
evaluation and user review of fixture realism remain required before submission.
